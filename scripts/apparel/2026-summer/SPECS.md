# Let's Pepper — Summer 2026 Team Kit · Heat Transfer Specs

Print art for sponsored Let's Pepper triples teams playing outside tournaments.
All files transparent PNG, 300 DPI metadata stamped. DTF (direct-to-film) heat
transfer — full color, flat inks, hard edges.

Design bar: Nino's Brand Kit pieces (`scripts/apparel/ref/`, originals from
~/Downloads/ref) — character + painted explosion + hand-brushed lettering as
ONE illustrated unit. Display lettering is never typeset; only the functional
payload (URL/handle) uses set type, in the clean-subline register of the
finished tee (Brand Kit-13).

Green colorway hero/lockup were generated multi-reference-locked on those
pieces (`tools/gen-images/scripts/lpo-apparel-kit.mjs`, gemini-pro), keyed and
upscaled into `scripts/apparel/assets/`. Assemble: `node scripts/apparel/render-apparel.mjs`.

Back print is the billboard (letspepper.com + @letspepper.open) — never drop it;
it's the entire point of sponsoring the team.

Dark garments only (black, forest, dark heather).
