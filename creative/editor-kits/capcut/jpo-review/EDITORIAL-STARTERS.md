to shorten selection time, not replace watching the originals. A/B ratings describe
editorial usefulness only. They do not identify teams, players, score, round, outcome,
or permissions.

## Five useful first edits

### 1. One point, no interruption

Use C2399 for the cleanest 4K anchor or C2386 for the strongest defense-to-reaction arc.
Let the rally play mostly at real speed. Slow only the decisive touch to 25%, then return
to real speed for the reaction. Use the `FULL RALLY` lower third on the setup or reset,
not over the touch.

### 2. JPO in motion

Open on C2398 or C2260, then move through C2318, C2353, C2374, and C2431. This gives
net action → serve → defense → net action → reaction. Keep the first cut under a second;
let the save and reaction breathe longer.

### 3. Serve rhythm

Cut C2318, C2359, C2401, and C2428 on toss/contact/body landing. Alternate real speed
and 25% slow motion. Do not stamp “ace” unless the full outcome is watched and verified.

### 4. Defense stays alive

Build around C2353 and C2386, with C2260 or C2300 as a short opener. Avoid mocking copy,
failure sounds, or freeze frames on the losing side. The athletic commitment is the story.

### 5. Above the tape

Use C2374, C2398, C2415, and the attacking beat in C2439. A restrained contact sound can
link the cuts. Do not call a clip a block, kill, or stuff until the touch and outcome are
confirmed in playback.

## How the current JPO graphics fit

- `generic/intro-reel.png` — visually strong, but use it after a cold action hook or for a
  dedicated team reel. A full-screen logo card is not the strongest first frame.
- `generic/outro-reel.png` — good single-job closer. Hold long enough to read the gallery
  address; do not add a second CTA.
- `generic/bug.png` — useful co-brand attribution, but too wide to remain prominent through
  an entire rally. Enter once, then reduce to roughly 18–24% opacity or use a compact bug.
- `generic/lowerthird-play.png` — now uses factual `JALAPEÑO OPEN / FULL RALLY` copy. Use
  only on a complete point.
- `generic/lowerthird-champions.png` and `lowerthird-finalists.png` — good after identity and
  result verification; place during setup or reaction.
- `teams/*-intro-reel.png` — appropriate for team features, not the general tournament recap.

The current design language is strongest when the anime mascot owns a title or chapter
beat. It becomes less effective when placed over live ball contact. The footage already
provides motion, stakes, texture, and credibility; mascot and typography should establish
identity, not compete with the play.
