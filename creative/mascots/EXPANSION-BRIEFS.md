# Mascot expansion: anchor briefs

These are v1 character anchors, not full pose packs. Each anchor must first prove a
repeatable editorial role. Generate signature action and reaction poses only after
the identity survives silhouette, anatomy, and tone review.

## Shared art direction

- Match `public/images/mascots/anime/family-overview.png`: mature gritty sports anime,
  controlled ink contours, sharp cel shading, crosshatched shadows, restrained grain,
  deep blacks, and glossy vegetable highlights.
- Exactly one full-body athlete with coherent anatomy, two arms, two legs, five-fingered
  hands, complete shoes, generous edge padding, and no crop.
- Black/charcoal performance kit, off-white socks, black court shoes. A volleyball is
  optional and must use the existing green/cream or red/cream panel language.
- Flat removable chroma only; no scene, floor, cast shadow, glow, aura, lettering,
  number, badge, sponsor mark, watermark, or extra character.
- Never drift toward chibi, Pixar/3D toy, rubber-hose, cute food mascot, superhero cape,
  photoreal human skin, or bodybuilder pin-up language.

## Identities and jobs

### Habanero — explosive outside hitter

Compact orange lantern silhouette; short green stem; powerful but mobile build; loaded
approach stance with one arm ready to swing. Useful for finishes, launches, and high-
energy campaign art. Intense, not angry for its own sake.

### Carolina Reaper — championship closer

Deep crimson, sharply wrinkled pod with an unmistakable lower stinger tail and dark
stem; lean, imposing athlete in a quiet ready stance. Reserved for finals, difficulty
tiers, milestones, and premium apparel. No gore, flame body, demon horns, skulls, or
horror imagery.

### Pepper X — experimental wildcard

Yellow-green, crumpled angular pod; irregular but readable silhouette; wiry agile build;
unconventional sky-ball wind-up or tactical ready stance. Useful for quiz results,
randomizers, predictions, and surprising plays. Strange, not sci-fi or “unhinged.”

### Chipotle — veteran captain

Smoky dark burgundy-brown dried jalapeño silhouette; composed older veteran in a black
warm-up top and shorts, carrying a ball at the hip. Useful for sportsmanship, history,
captaincy, season recaps, and awards. No smoke cloud, cigar, cowboy, sombrero, weapon,
or cultural costume shorthand.

### Pepper Belle — elite setter/captain

Deep red-to-purple bell pepper silhouette; powerful female-presenting athlete in a
non-revealing charcoal racerback kit; hands set in a clean setter-ready triangle.
Useful for the women's series, assists, leadership, and registrations. No crown,
princess cues, makeup exaggeration, pin-up pose, tiny waist, cleavage, or pink coding.

### Pepperoncini — coach/commentator

Pale yellow-green curved pepper; experienced mentor in a black track jacket and pants,
holding a clipboard with an optional minimal headset. Useful for rules, FAQ, schedules,
weather, explainers, and commentary. Knowledgeable, not elderly caricature.

### Banana Pepper — setter/strategist

Long curved yellow pepper; lean agile athlete in a black kit; setter-ready stance with
hands raised and focused eyes. Useful for assists, explainers, schedules, polls, and
lighter editorial beats. Confident, not a slapstick banana character.

### Shishito — utility wildcard

Slim wrinkled dark-green pepper; compact agile athlete in a low ready stance with a
restrained knowing expression. Useful for upset alerts, utility plays, weekly wildcard
posts, quizzes, and bracket surprises. Composed, not clownish or chaotic.

## Chroma plan

- Orange/red/burgundy characters: flat `#00ff00` green.
- Green/yellow-green/yellow characters: flat `#ff00ff` magenta.
- Chroma removal uses the repository-copied source and the imagegen skill's
  `remove_chroma_key.py`; alpha edges must be inspected against black and white.
